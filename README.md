# Translate the Words

This is an open-ended translation challenge designed to help users quickly test and reinforce their knowledge of English vocabulary.

The words are sourced from the New General Service List (NGSL), and their translations were obtained using Google services.

The quiz presents a list of 30 words to be translated in a single session.

After entering all your answers, press the "Check-out" button to activate immediate correction. Each answer is compared against the exact solution, ignoring case, capitalization, and excess spaces.

Finally, the interface displays your final score and instantly reveals the correct solutions for every word.
